## Full description  (max 4000)

```
One on One is a private messaging app built for a single relationship at a time.

There is no contact list, no groups, no feed, no discovery, and no "new chat"
button. You sign in with Google, get one Connection ID, share it with one person
however you like, and once they accept, the two of you have exactly one
conversation. Opening the app takes you straight into it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Text, with replies, emoji reactions, and read receipts
• Send photos, voice notes, and files
• Audio and video calls
• Write a "letter" — a longer, slower message styled like a note
• Small shared moments: check-ins, countdowns, this-or-that, a nudge alarm
• Share a one-off location

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• Message content is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. All traffic is over
  HTTPS/WSS.
• No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. Nothing is sold or shared for
  marketing.
• We don't store your IP address or a device fingerprint.
• Not end-to-end encrypted — the server processes messages to deliver them.
  See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and for how long.

SAFETY
• Block & end: instantly ends the connection and permanently stops that person
  from contacting you again.
• Report a message or a person, including a child-safety category. Reports are
  reviewed by a real person.
• Delete your account any time — in the app or from a web page — which removes
  your account and your conversation.

One on One is for adults (18+). It is a communication tool, not a social
network, and it is meant to stay that way.

Privacy policy: https://one-on-one-mu.vercel.app/privacy
Terms of service: https://one-on-one-mu.vercel.app/terms
Child safety: https://one-on-one-mu.vercel.app/child-safety
```
