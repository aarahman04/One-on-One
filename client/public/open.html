Opening from an Instagram Story?

If the button does nothing, tap Instagram's three-dot menu and choose Open in browser or Open in Chrome (wording varies). Then tap “Open in Google Play app” again. This avoids the Google sign-in page inside Instagram.

This link does not install anything automatically. Instagram may prevent external apps from opening inside its browser.
